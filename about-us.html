About EmojiFace.US

The story behind the best advanced face AI tool to cover face with various emoji

Our Story

EmojiFace.US began in 2023 when our founder, a developer and emoji enthusiast, faced a common challenge: finding a way to mask faces in photos while maintaining the emotional context of the original image.

After experimenting with various AI technologies and facial recognition systems, they created a simple tool that could detect faces and replace them with emotionally-matched emojis. What started as a personal project quickly evolved into EmojiFace.US - a free service designed to help anyone protect their privacy while creating fun, shareable content.

Today, our platform serves thousands of users daily, offering instant face-to-emoji transformation with our advanced face AI technology and a growing collection of unique Labubu emojis that you won't find anywhere else.

Our Mission

"To make face privacy protection simple, fast, and accessible to everyone, while empowering creative digital expression through our advanced face AI technology."

At EmojiFace.US, we believe that privacy and creativity can go hand in hand. Our mission is to provide the simplest, fastest way to mask faces with emojis and Labubu emoji that can be used anywhere - from social media posts and messaging apps to educational materials and digital content.

We're committed to:

  • Privacy First: Processing all photos locally in your browser - your images never leave your device
  • Accessibility: Keeping our core services free and available to everyone
  • Innovation: Continuously improving our face AI technology and expanding our emoji collection
  • Quality: Maintaining the highest standards of image processing and emoji matching
